It's quite famous due to...

Caesar Cipher is a fairly popular substitution cipher where each letter in the plaintext is replaced with a letter some fixed number of positions down the alphabet.

	The key is the number of positions to shift the alphabet.

	Example:
	Plaintext: HELLO
	Shift: 3
	Ciphertext: KHOOR
